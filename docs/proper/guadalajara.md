On the universal calendar, not on this one: 27 Apr 2026 is Peter Canisius (`Sancti/04-27.txt`, rank 3). Turibius is not moved to that day. 23 Nov stays Clement at rank 3, because p. 53 prints no class and this version does not replace that file.

On this version, 5 Feb is Philip, and Agatha is commemorated, because a 3rd class particular feast precedes a universal 3rd class. 18 Dec remains the Advent feria, with the Expectation commemorated. 25 Feb 2026 is Ember Wednesday and commemorates Sebastian. 2 Mar 2026 is a Lenten feria and commemorates Gutierrez. 23 Mar 2016 is Holy Wednesday and omits Turibius. 16 May 2027 is Pentecost and omits Nepomucene. 30 Aug 2026, a Sunday, keeps Rose and commemorates the Sunday. 12 Dec 2027, Advent Sunday, keeps the Sunday. General Rubrics nn. 95-96 transfer Guadalupe, a 1st class feast, to 13 December, which is not a 1st or 2nd class day. No octave was added.

Feasts that say the whole Office is from the common, with no further text, use that common. Where the book prints an oration, hymn, antiphon, or lesson, that printing is what the file uses. The I Vespers collect of Rose prints `un in odórem`. That is the letter on the page, not a broken `ut`. A later repetition of the same collect prints `ut`. The file keeps the first printing. The first Guadalupe lesson is marked `Lectio j` on the page: the letter has a descender. Under Rubrics 1960 the generator displays that `j` as `i`, and the lesson itself is Lectio 1, not a missing lesson. `meis mitte radíces` is what the page prints. The `tt` is a ligature the text extractor had dropped; it is restored. Other ligatures are expanded only to the same letters.

Martin keeps the breviary Office and takes the printed II class. Jan 24, Oct 12, and the cathedral dedication name the common and print no proper collect. Mass is not offered.
