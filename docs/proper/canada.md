# Canada — draft, not selectable

Status: **incomplete draft**. Nothing in this page is a menu. The loader does not read it. No Office propers are generated. This is not a complete national calendar.

Desired composition, when a later pull request activates a real calendar, and not before:

`Rubrics 1960` → country `canada` → shared FSSP institute layer **without** `usa` → diocese → apostolate.

The FSSP version that runs today stays `usa,fssp`. A Canadian apostolate must not inherit that USA layer.

Records: [`proposals/country/canada/`](../../web/www/Tabulae/Propria/proposals/country/canada/_layer.txt).

## National candidates

Source for these four lines: the owner-approved FSSP *2026 Ordo*, North American edition (Fraternity Publications). Page numbers are the printed pages. *Officia Propria Canadensis* (1962) and the Canadian missal supplement are named by that ordo and are not in this repository. Ranks below are the ordo's classes, not engine numbers.

| 12-12 | Our Lady of Guadalupe | 3 | White, Gloria, preface of the Blessed Virgin. pp. 12–13 and 40–41. Marked CAN and USA. | [12-12](../../web/www/Tabulae/Propria/proposals/country/canada/12-12.txt) |

The ordo gives no separate Office lessons or hymns for these days. `office_text` stays pending. No Sancti file is added.

Jan 12 and the 2026 Ember Saturday on Sep 26 are observations of those printed pages. They are not new rubrics. Guadalupe's 1962 status (*ubi conceditur* in the proper the ordo cites) is not the same question as the ordo's citation of a Congregation text dated 25 March 1999. That later text is not here. The draft does not decide between them, and it enters no octave.

Holy days of obligation named on p. 7 are Jan 1 and Dec 25. That is discipline, not rank.

These USA-only days are not copied into the Canada layer: Jan 4, Jan 5, Jan 22 votive Mass for Peace, Sep 9, the USA 3rd-class line on Sep 26, Oct 25, Nov 13.

The ordo is not a full index of the 1962 Canadian proper. Days it does not mark CAN, including several saints sometimes discussed for Canada, are not added.

## Dioceses — not this layer

Printed only as diocesan or provincial notes on pp. 53–54. They are not files and not a national calendar. Ranks are the classes printed there.

- Calgary: Oct 16 St. Gerard Majella, secondary patron, II; Dec 8 Immaculate Conception, principal patroness, I; Dec 11 cathedral dedication, I. The II class on Oct 16 outranks the national III class of that date. That clash is not resolved here.
- Edmonton: May 1 cathedral dedication, I.
- Ottawa: Mar 17 St. Patrick, patron, I; Jun 24 Nativity of St. John the Baptist, patron, I; Sep 4 cathedral dedication, I.
- St. Catharines: Nov 25 St. Catharine of Alexandria, principal patroness, I; Nov 26 cathedral dedication, transferred from Nov 25, I.
- Vancouver: Oct 3 cathedral dedication, I; Oct 7 Our Lady of the Holy Rosary, principal patroness, I.
- Montréal: May 31 Queenship of Mary, principal feast, I; Sep 12 Holy Name of Mary, principal patroness of the ecclesiastical province of Québec, I (provincial, not national); Oct 13 cathedral dedication, I.
- Québec: Jul 11 cathedral dedication, I; Jul 26 St. Anne, principal patroness of the archdiocese, I. Not national.
- Saskatoon: May 13 cathedral dedication, I; Jun 30 St. Paul, principal patron, I. The ordo prints "Archdiocese" for Saskatoon. That word is left as printed.
